## What it is and for whom

Hand-designing a nonogram with exactly one purely logical solution is tedious and error-prone, and a random black/white grid rarely has that property. The Nonogram Generator produces puzzles from a random grid, a built-in image library or the user's own image, at a chosen difficulty, and guarantees each one has exactly one logical solution. It serves one actor, the Puzzle Creator, who generates, tunes and exports puzzles for printing, solving and sharing. Business model (vision.md): a personal/hobby tool, not commercial.
