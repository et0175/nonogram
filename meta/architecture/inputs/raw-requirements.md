Source: independent post-hoc review of CARD-019 (meta/review/20260830T163436Z-CARD-019-posthoc.yml, findings F-001/F-002), 2026-08-30.

# processed: 2026-08-31 → EC-007 (wording correction)
- AMENDS EC-007: its closing clause "refused before any conversion, dithering or solver work runs" is literally false on one path and must be narrowed to what the implementation can guarantee — "refused before any cropping, dithering or solver work runs". Loading and greyscaling are NOT refusal-free in general. Where a source's header extent and its decoded extent disagree, the refusal necessarily comes after `load_greyscale`, because the extent being judged is the one only the decode knows. Two disagreement classes measured on Pillow 12.3.0, CARD-026 review cycle 2, 2026-08-31: a PNG whose `eXIf` chunk follows `IDAT` (563x980 stored, 980x563 displayed — `Image.open` never reaches the chunk), and an orientation-tagged TIFF (60x40 + `tiffinfo={274: 6}` probes (40,60), decodes (60,40) — Pillow's TIFF reader applies the orientation at open and `ImageOps.exif_transpose` applies it a second time). The two-stage guard that creates this path is what makes CON-012 hold unconditionally: judging the header extent alone accepted a 15x30 request that retained only 0.287 of the displayed picture, a silent fail-OPEN. Cost of the narrowing, swept over all 441 grid shapes against the 563x980/980x563 disagreement: 105 accepted, 168 refused after the decode (the correct outcome), 168 false refusals — fail-CLOSED, each carrying an actionable message. Everything downstream of the decode (cropping, dithering, clue derivation, the solver) remains unreachable for every refused request, so the guarantee that matters is intact and only the word "conversion" was too broad (EC, correction). Source: meta/review/20260831T121656Z-CARD-026-cycle2.yml finding F-102, 2026-08-31.

# processed: 2026-09-01 -> FR-023, US-021
