## Why this card exists

ADR-0042/R1 says puzzle pages, answer pages, the TOC and the guide are PDF content-stream operators on every export path. `book_pdf_generator._RASTER_DECLARATIONS` declares five temporary raster exceptions, each `expires_with=("CARD-299", "CARD-322")`: `PageKind.TITLE`, `GUIDE`, `TOC`, `ANSWER`, `PUZZLE`. CARD-299 is done, so **this card cannot be marked `done` while any of the five remains**; `test_no_declaration_outlives_the_cards_that_remove_it` enforces it. This card removes them, builds the download path through the assembly point (`book_interior_vector.interior_generator`), flips R1, **deletes the bitmap interior path that the flip leaves with no caller** (item 16), retires the tests whose only subject was a raster interior page (item 17), and adds stored vector goldens in place of the pixel baselines the interior loses (item 18). The cover and the section divider stay raster (CARD-231 replaces the divider). It also carries, as its first two commits, the two small open test cards that would otherwise block it (CARD-342, CARD-350).
