## Why this card exists

ADR-0042/R1 says puzzle pages, answer pages, the TOC and the guide are PDF content-stream operators on every export path. `book_pdf_generator._RASTER_DECLARATIONS` declares five temporary raster exceptions, each `expires_with=("CARD-299", "CARD-322")`: `PageKind.TITLE`, `GUIDE`, `TOC`, `ANSWER`, `PUZZLE`. CARD-299 is done, so **this card cannot be marked `done` while any of the five remains**; `test_no_declaration_outlives_the_cards_that_remove_it` enforces it. This card removes them, builds the download path through the assembly point (`book_interior_vector.interior_generator`), flips R1 with the last conversion, and retires the tests whose only subject was a raster interior page. The cover and the section divider stay raster (CARD-231 replaces the divider).
