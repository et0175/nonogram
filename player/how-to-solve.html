How to solve a nonogram

Step 1 of 7 · The rules

The rules

The numbers beside each row and above each column tell you the runs of black squares in that line, in order, with at least one white square between runs. A row marked 4 2 has a run of four, a gap, then a run of two.

Try one

Squeeze from both ends

Slide a run as far left as it will go, then as far right. Every square it might cover is a maybe. Any square covered both times must be black. A row of 10 with the clue 8:

Six squares solved without knowing where the run starts. Come back to this after every discovery: the more you know, the tighter the squeeze.

Keep what every arrangement agrees on

Think of every way the clue could fit the line, given what you already know. A square black in all of them is black. A square empty in all of them is white.

Marking whites matters: most of the time it is a white square that unlocks the next line. This idea contains the squeeze; it is the same idea taken all the way.

Probe, and watch it break

Stuck? Press Probe, make one open square black, and follow what it forces. Everything you mark while probing gets a dashed edge. If a line can no longer fit its clue, press ✗ Wrong guess: all the dashed marks go away, and the page offers to mark that first square the other colour — press Mark it. If the probe solves the puzzle instead, press ✓ Keep.

This is not guessing: you prove one option impossible, so the other one is certain.

Try a probe

The buttons

Every puzzle has these, and the same list sits under each board as How to play.

  • Tap a cell to fill it. Tap again to cycle: black, white (dot), empty.
  • Brush (the square): choose what a tap puts down: Black, White, Maybe (?) or Clear.
  • Region (the two squares): turn it on, then drag along a row or column to mark many cells at once. With it off, dragging scrolls.
  • Hint: press up to three times. First it shows where to look, then why, then it fills the cell.
  • Probe: try a guess safely. Keep it if it works out, or press Wrong guess to undo it all.
  • − +Zoom: − / + or Fit to see the whole puzzle.

Big puzzles on a phone

On a puzzle 20 squares or more on a side, the squares on a phone are smaller than a fingertip. Press Line, beside Hint, to open one row or column in big squares, with its clue above it.

Tap the small board, or use the arrows, to pick a line, and Rows / Cols to switch direction. A long line is split into two strips. Tap or drag along a strip to mark it, with the same Black, White and Maybe as the board. Press and hold a square to jump to the line that crosses it there.

Every mark goes straight onto the board, and Undo works as usual. Probe works here too, with Keep and Wrong guess. Press Board to go back to the whole puzzle.

What the levels mean

Easy
Squeezing from both ends is enough, start to finish.
Medium
Needs what every arrangement agrees on, and may need a probe for part of the grid.
Hard
Needs probing over most of the grid.

Every puzzle here has exactly one solution. You never have to guess, and there is never a second answer.
